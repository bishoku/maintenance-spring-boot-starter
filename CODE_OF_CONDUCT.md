# Code of conduct

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, constructive and respectful.

- Treat people with respect regardless of background, identity or experience.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and critique ideas rather than people.
- Respect privacy; do not publish personal information without permission.
- Do not engage in harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct or spam.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests and other project-maintained
community spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions,
or restrict participation in response to violations, considering severity and context.

Report concerns privately to **contact@bishokudev.com**. Reports will be handled with care
and shared only as needed to investigate and respond. The same expectations apply to maintainers.
